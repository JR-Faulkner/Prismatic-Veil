A+T UI Sound Test

Mechanical Behemoth2 UI cues now used by the live A+T sound helper.

SelectionLight switch click for normal buttons, claims, and list selections.
Positive Confirm / Do ItApproved relay actuator: command accepted, tight and repeatable.
NavigationSmall panel step for tabs, route changes, start, continue, and home links.
Reject / BlockedLower failed-action clunk for disabled or unresolved actions.
End DayApproved shift-close latch: heavier commit, wind-down, final relay.

These are generated in-browser with the same cue shapes as assets/js/prpg-audio.js. Use phone speakers for the real pass.
