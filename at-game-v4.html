✦ THE VERDANT

A respectable business. A questionable reason.

In your family, powers are kept small. Party tricks. Family stories. Useful little oddities that sensible people do not build a life around. The uncle who ignored that rule supposedly went crazy. Lately, you have started wondering whether that story was ever true.

You told them it was salvage.

Your new Alteration ability feels almost useless on paper: things that are yours become strangely legible, and you can change them. But every time you use it, a calm warmth settles through you with a flicker of approval and joy. So you asked the family for seed money to start a blue-collar recovery and salvage operation near the Verdant. They heard “honest work.” You heard “a frontier full of abandoned things, uncertain claims, and questions nobody at home wants asked.”
