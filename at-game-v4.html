✦ THE VERDANT

You did not come here to become normal.

Your family has powers. They just learned not to use them. A spark at a cookout. A strange little trick at a birthday. Something useful once in a while, then back to being ordinary before anybody notices too much. The one relative who refused that rule became the family warning: your uncle “went crazy.” You are starting to suspect he simply kept going.

The salvage business is the part you told them.

Alteration awakened quietly. Anything you truly think of as yours becomes strangely legible, almost writable. Every use brings a deep calm, a warm pulse of approval, and the same impossible question: why would anyone spend a lifetime refusing this? Your family gave you seed money for a respectable recovery and salvage operation. You used it to move to the Verdant, where old roads vanish into green, ownership gets fuzzy, government reach gets thin, and the unknown still has room to breathe.
